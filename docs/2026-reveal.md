# The October 2026 redesign

The reveal took place on **October 15, 2026, in McGregor, Texas**. That was October 16 in Sweden. Event witnesses describe an unmanned prototype driving inverted and performing airborne maneuvers. This is evidence of a demonstration, not of a delivered, certified production vehicle. [Reuters](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/teslas-new-roadster-turns-air-spacex-thrusters-wont-land-until-2028) · [Top Gear’s eyewitness report](https://www.topgear.com/car-news/first-look/wow-new-tesla-roadster-can-actually-fly)

## Configurations on Tesla’s current page

| Configuration | Seats | Equipment distinction | Tesla’s availability wording |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Performance | 2 | Active aero and fan suction | By reservation |
| Thrust | 2 | Adds rear acceleration thrusters | Limited release |
| Rocket | 1 | Adds perimeter thrusters for airborne movement | Invite only; not approved for public road use |

Source: [Tesla product page](https://www.tesla.com/roadster), directly read October 16. Tesla calls Thrust and Rocket **augments** in parts of its site. That does not establish the final order structure. Footnotes restrict some features to Rocket and describe optional Thrust equipment; avoid applying every visual or performance claim to every configuration.

## Keep the old and new evidence apart

| Topic | 2017 concept | 2026 evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Range | Advertised 620 miles | Reported event claim above 300 miles; final certification unresolved |
| Cabin | Advertised four seats | Two or one, depending on configuration |
| Battery capacity | Proposed 200 kWh | Final usable capacity not disclosed in the reviewed interview |
| Drivetrain | Three motors in launch-era reporting | Twin rear motors described in the engineering interview |
| Launch timing | Originally expected in 2020 | Current reported production target: 2028 |

Sources: [Legacy Tesla page snapshot](https://www.tesla.com/roadster?lang=en), [2017 launch report](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2017/11/not-just-a-semi-announcement-tesla-promises-a-new-roadster/), [engineering interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=22rvpUecLts&t=161s), [Reuters](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/teslas-new-roadster-turns-air-spacex-thrusters-wont-land-until-2028).

## What to do with the acceleration figures

The exact numbers vary across the interview, event demonstration and reporting. Read the [measurement guide](testing.md) and [timestamped interview notes](../research/interview-notes.md). We do not present the smallest published number as a verified production-car record.

## What this edition cannot establish

Final retail price, usable battery capacity, full charging curve, curb mass by configuration, certified range, complete flight envelope and customer delivery date remain unresolved. These gaps are collected in [open questions](open-questions.md), with the evidence that could close each one.
