## A deposit is not a car price

On October 16, the **US configurator** displayed $5,000 immediately plus $45,000 within ten days for Performance, and $5,000 plus $245,000 for Thrust. Rocket was invite only. These were reservation payments, not a published retail price. [Tesla reservation page](https://www.tesla.com/roadster/reserve)

The [linked US agreement](https://www.tesla.com/coinorder/api/v4/terms?locale=en_US&model=mt&saleType=PreSale) was recovered in the follow-up review. Both pages identify **US v.20261015**. Clause 5 permits either party to cancel with a full Reservation Payment refund until the customer completes configuration after Tesla's invitation. Clause 4 guarantees neither delivery timing nor eventual delivery. Clause 6 says funds are not held separately in escrow/trust, earn no interest and require prior written consent to transfer the reservation.

Clause 10 contains individual arbitration, a small-claims alternative and a written opt-out within 30 days after signing, subject to the full clause's conditions. These describe the document's wording, not an enforceability determination. The interface also restricts refunds for modifications; its interaction with later configuration/order terms remains unresolved. Incorporated policies and later contracts are not fully reviewed. See the [clause-by-clause document review](../research/document-review.md). No reservation was made. Other markets and agreements can differ.

## Prototype, production and delivery are separate milestones

A public demonstration shows a prototype can perform the demonstrated task under its setup. Series production requires a repeatable manufacturing process. Delivery requires an actual customer handover. Tesla’s reported 2028 production guidance establishes a target, not the latter two milestones. [Reuters](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/teslas-new-roadster-turns-air-spacex-thrusters-wont-land-until-2028)

## United States

Tesla’s current page specifically states that Rocket is not approved for public-road use. Do not interpret the label “road ready” for another configuration as a completed certification record. [Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/roadster)

US vehicle compliance generally uses manufacturer self-certification against applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards; it is misleading to describe that entire process as NHTSA preapproving each new car. NHTSA provides the [official certification overview](https://www.nhtsa.gov/importing-vehicle/importation-and-certification-faqs) and [manufacturer resources](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-manufacturers).

A road-vehicle compliance route does not itself establish permission for airborne operation. The [FAA’s advanced-air-mobility resources](https://www.faa.gov/air-taxis) are background only. No Roadster aircraft classification or aviation approval is established here.

## European Union and Sweden

The EU has a vehicle type-approval framework under [Regulation (EU) 2018/858](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/858/oj/eng). The [Swedish Transport Agency explains type approval](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/en/road/Vehicles/type-approval/what-is-a-type-approval/), including whole vehicles and components. Neither link proves approval of a Roadster variant. Consult current consolidated requirements and the responsible authority for an actual vehicle or use case.

## Questions a future customer would still need answered

Final contract price; local eligibility; allowed modes on road and track; operating restrictions; insurance; charging and nitrogen-refill support; inspection intervals; consumable skirts; service and warranty terms; and actual delivery commitments. This repository tracks evidence, not purchase recommendations or a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.
