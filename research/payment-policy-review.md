# Payments and refunds after configuration

[Home](../README.md) · [Reservation context](../docs/availability.md) · [Agreement review](document-review.md) · [Open questions](../docs/open-questions.md)

Checked **October 16, 2026**, for the public US reservation flow. **Post-configuration refund rights remain unresolved.** Reading the incorporated policies closes an access gap; it does not supply the later Roadster Order or Purchase Agreement.

## Follow the incorporation chain

Clause 7 of [S41, US v.20261015](https://www.tesla.com/coinorder/api/v4/terms?locale=en_US&model=mt&saleType=PreSale) incorporates Tesla's Privacy Notice and Payment Terms of Use Policy and points to the legal hub. The [US configurator, S02](https://www.tesla.com/roadster/reserve) links directly to [Payment Terms, S51](https://www.tesla.com/legal/terms?redirect=no#payment-terms) and [Customer Privacy Notice, S52](https://www.tesla.com/legal/privacy?redirect=no). Those destinations were opened in the browser rather than inferred from search previews.

The payment-policy review covers **Payment Terms → General and Vehicle Charging Policy**. The same webpage also contains separate rideshare, Robotaxi and Supercharger fair-use sections. Their cancellation or arbitration clauses must not silently be treated as Roadster reservation terms. The reservation agreement has its own dispute clause.

## What each stage establishes

| Stage or action | Verified wording and limit |
|---|---|
| Reservation before completing configuration following Tesla's invitation | Agreement clause 5 expressly provides either-party cancellation through the Tesla Account with a full Reservation Payment refund. It specifies original-payment routing and a named-holder check fallback, but gives no numerical processing deadline. |
| Completing configuration and proceeding to order | Clause 3 anticipates an Order Agreement, updated agreements for configuration changes, and a final Purchase Agreement before delivery. These later Roadster documents were not obtained in this review. |
| Requesting a modification | The live interface says “Refunds for modifications will not be issued.” It does not define modifications, identify the affected amount, or explain the relation to cancellation. |
| Cancelling a bank-debit authorization | Payment Terms → General gives a 30-day notice mechanism and discusses recourse for unauthorized debits. This addresses payment authorization, not an express Roadster order-cancellation/refund entitlement. |
| Stopping recurring service charges | The same policy describes a 24-hour-before-renewal cutoff for supported recurring charges. It does not identify the Roadster reservation as a subscription. |
| Requesting deletion of personal information | Privacy rights concern data handling, subject to applicable conditions and retention requirements. They do not state a Roadster refund entitlement. |

The configurator also displays a **Fully refundable** label in its payment table. Preserve that observation alongside clause 5's express window and the modification restriction; do not turn the label into an unlimited, post-configuration guarantee. Conversely, expiry of the express window is not itself a written declaration that every payment is forfeited. Clause 8's remedy limit also does not, on its own, promise a refund for every later cancellation.

## What the incorporated policies add

The Payment Terms address stored methods, collection, bank debits and recurring charges. They allow amendments effective on posting. Their charging subsection addresses charging-service fees and holds. **No Roadster-specific post-configuration refund, forfeiture schedule or definition of modification was found in these reviewed payment sections.** This is a finding about this evidence set, not a claim that no applicable provision exists elsewhere. [S51](https://www.tesla.com/legal/terms?redirect=no#payment-terms)

The Privacy Notice's expanded collection, use, sharing and choice sections describe financial/order data, payment processors and retention criteria. It displays **Updated February 2026**. It supplies data-handling context rather than the missing vehicle cancellation schedule. This review does not verify Tesla's implementation of those practices. [S52](https://www.tesla.com/legal/privacy?redirect=no)

## What remains unknown, and what would resolve it

- **The amount refundable after configuration:** obtain the actual US Roadster Order Agreement and final Purchase Agreement applicable to the customer, including configuration-change and cancellation provisions.
- **The meaning of modification:** obtain attributable written clarification distinguishing a trim change, price reduction, augmentation removal and cancellation, and identifying any amount retained.
- **When configuration is complete:** obtain the invitation, acceptance flow and dated confirmation. This review did not perform those steps or establish whether today's reserve-page selections constitute that later contractual milestone.
- **Timing and exceptions:** obtain a stated refund-processing deadline and applicable provisions for Tesla cancellation, development delays, non-delivery and customer cancellation after the cutoff.
- **Which policy version and jurisdiction apply:** preserve the signed agreement and incorporated policy versions at acceptance, plus later accepted amendments. Today's mutable public pages do not prove a particular earlier customer's terms. Individual statutory rights, enforceability and remedies were not determined here.

No applicable later Roadster order/purchase contract was located in the bounded public-source search. Results included stale agreement previews, an original-generation US reservation form, a 2017 Australian Roadster form and other models' regional contracts. None was substituted for the current US Roadster's later terms. Search-result ages were not treated as contract version dates.
