# Research method and limits

Edition cutoff: **2026-10-16**. Language: English. Scope: the redesigned Tesla Roadster, its 2017 predecessor concept, essential original-Roadster history and relevant engineering/regulatory context.

## How this edition was assembled

Searches covered Tesla product/reservation material, Tesla video records, engineering interviews, recent launch reporting, selected earlier announcements, patent records, NASA/DOE physics references, and official US/EU/Swedish regulatory resources. The working folder was empty at intake; there was no pre-existing Tesla codebase to audit.

The current Tesla product page and US reservation options were read directly in a browser. This mattered because a direct HTTP request was denied and the search index still returned the legacy concept page. Public interview captions and publisher descriptions were inspected for the Hagerty engineering interview. Source records state where access was only indexed, partial, blocked or metadata-only.

We retained short factual paraphrases and links. We did not mirror articles, download/rehost copyrighted videos or photos, or acquire unpublished material. Original charts and calculations are analytical teaching aids, not measured vehicle data.

## Evidence labels

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| observed-page | The indicated interface/text was directly inspected; not proof of the vehicle’s capability |
| manufacturer-claim | Attributable Tesla assertion, without independent performance validation |
| interview-claim | Vehicle detail attributable to the reviewed executive interview; still provisional |
| reported-claim | An outlet attributes a claim; primary performance evidence not independently checked |
| reported-observation | A reporter describes witnessing the event; no independent test protocol implied |
| reported-interview | Secondary interview summary; direct review incomplete for that detail |
| conflicting-reports | Figures/definitions differ and reconciliation is incomplete; not necessarily a factual contradiction |
| historical-claim | A past announcement or proposal, not a present specification |
| patent-disclosure | Content disclosed in a patent, not confirmed production fitment |
| unknown | Not established by this bounded research pass |

Source access is separate from claim confidence. A fully readable article may repeat a weak claim. A manufacturer page is primary evidence of what the manufacturer says, not independent proof that it is true. Multiple outlets repeating one statement are not multiple independent measurements.

## Dates and citations

Event date, publication date and checked date are different fields. Approximate dates retain their precision, such as `2012-01`. Future timeline entries are explicitly targets. A null publication date means not established, not “published on the checked date.” Sources can update in place; the checked date is not an immutable archived snapshot.

Use claim IDs for corrections. Prefer the original source and a timestamp or section locator. Preserve older claims as history when they are superseded. Do not silently edit an earlier promise into a newer one. The machine-readable ledger is canonical; generated Markdown tables and the explorer are derived.

## Limits that remain

- This is curated research, not “all information on the internet.” There is no guarantee of exhaustive historical, regional or non-English coverage.
- No vehicle was tested, no customer delivery verified, and no regulator-specific clearance established.
- Captions can mistranscribe units and names. Ambiguous phrases are flagged, not converted into precise engineering specifications.
- The complete Top Gear companion video, blocked X post, full reservation agreement and two primary patent documents remain incompletely reviewed. Relevant official-event and Hagerty transcript passages were reviewed; a complete audiovisual review was not performed.
- No automatic monitoring is installed. Dates and status require a deliberate new source review.
- Content and code were initially assembled with AI assistance and checked against the listed sources; independent subject-matter review remains welcome.
